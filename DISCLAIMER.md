## What this is

A research project about how a small classification model (TypeSafe's Jev)
behaves inside a decision system, using prediction markets as the test bed.
It is published for **educational and research purposes only**. It is not a
product, a service, or a trading system anyone should rely on.

on or trade anything. The findings describe a few days of small-sample
experiments; the project's own write-up (docs/FINDINGS.md) states that no
edge was demonstrated. Past results, paper results and backtests say nothing
reliable about future results.
